ABOUT SKY CONSULTANCY

Compliance is more than paperwork.

Sky Consultancy Management & Services provides professional compliance and business advisory with a primary focus on Malaysia's construction industry.

Since beginning operations in 2023, we have supported contractors and businesses in navigating CIDB requirements, regulatory procedures and operational compliance through practical, solution-oriented advisory.
